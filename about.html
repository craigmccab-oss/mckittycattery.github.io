❤️ Welcome to Mc Kitty Cattery! 🐱

Thank you for considering us for your beloved cat's stay! We're thrilled to welcome your furry friend to our warm and welcoming cat boarding facility. Our dedicated team is committed to providing a comfortable, enjoyable, and stress-free experience for your cat while you're away.

Our facility features

1
Spacious and cozy accommodations with undercover and breezy secure enclosures
2
Vertical shelving for climbing and exploration
3
Semi-undercover catio with fresh air and cozy sleeping spaces
4
Regular meals, fresh water, and plenty of affection and playtime

Booking Process

To secure your cat's reservation, please:

1
Complete the attached documentation, including our Terms and Conditions
2
Provide a scanned copy of your cat's Sterilization & Vaccination Card (cover to cover)
3
Return the signed forms for review and confirmation
4
Pay the advance payment (or 50% deposit) to finalize and secure the booking
